# **About Me**

Hi, I am **{{ site.author.name }}** — an Information/Data Science graduate of the
**University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**, with a minor in Data Science and an
Information Trust Institute Certificate in Cybersecurity.

I work in Python and SQL to clean data, run exploratory and statistical analysis, test
hypotheses, and turn the results into visualizations and conclusions people can actually
use. Alongside that, I spent two years supporting university technology — troubleshooting
wired and wireless networks, resolving software tickets, and maintaining computer labs,
including UIUC's XR Lab. That mix has given me a well-rounded perspective: technical depth
paired with the customer-service instinct to explain a problem in plain language.

I am motivated by building secure, efficient technical environments and by collaborative
problem solving.
